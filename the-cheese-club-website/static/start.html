What happens when a queue fills

The bot guides you, and you can do every step in Discord or on the match page.

  1. 01 TEAMSCaptains draft the teams.
  2. 02 MAP1v1 players ban maps in turn until one is left.
  3. 03 CIV BANSEach side bans civs, hidden until both lock in.
  4. 04 CIV PICKSEveryone picks a civ, revealed together.
  5. 05 PLAYStart the match, play, and the captains report the result.

Want something bigger? Try a tournament

Tournaments can be limited to a rating range, so newer players meet players of a similar level. Tournament games are shown in match history but never change your club ranking.

1v1 Cup

Single elimination with best-of series, seeded by rating. Last duelist standing.

Team Cup

A captain registers a fixed team of 2, 3 or 4 and invites teammates. Single elimination.

All Random

Sign up alone. Every round you get new, balanced teammates and score individual points. The best way to meet people.

Free for all

2 to 8 players in one lobby, with points by final placement.

Good to know

Do I need to be in voice to queue?

Yes. Join any Cheese Club voice channel first, so the match can start the moment the queue fills.

Why link my AoE4World profile?

It's how the club knows your rating, so teams and tournament brackets are balanced fairly.

Can I do everything in Discord?

Queueing and the match steps work in both places. Tournament sign-up currently happens on this site.
