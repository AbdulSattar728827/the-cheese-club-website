About The Cheese Club

Who we are, what you can do here and how to get started

Strategy, friendship and a healthy respect for cheese.

The Cheese Club is an Age of Empires IV community on Discord. We run our own community matches, keep track of every result, and spend a lot of evenings together in voice.

Whether you're still learning your first civilization or chasing a higher rating, you'll find people to play with, learn from and lose to gracefully. And yes, we know what cheese means in AoE. A well-timed tower rush is always welcome here.

Winning is great and improving is better, but the people you play with are what keep you coming back.

What you can do here

  • Community matches

    Queue for 1v1 up to 4v4 games against other members, with civilization bans and every result recorded. See recent matches

  • Live leaderboards

    Your wins, losses and official AoE4World ratings, ranked alongside everyone else. View the leaderboard

  • Voice channels

    There's often someone around for a game or just to hang out. Members have spent — hours in voice together so far.

  • Tournaments Soon

    Community tournaments with brackets and results are on the way.
