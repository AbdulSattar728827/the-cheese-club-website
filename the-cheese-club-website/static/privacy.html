Privacy

What this website shows about members, and how to change it

Last updated: 13 November 2026

The Cheese Club website is a public, read-only view of activity in our Age of Empires IV community on Discord. This page explains what it shows, where that information comes from, and what you can ask us to change.

What the website shows

  • Your Discord display name and profile picture, as they appear in our server.
  • A shortened version of your Discord ID, such as 4246**************. Your full Discord ID is never shown on the website or sent to visitors' browsers.
  • If you linked your Age of Empires IV account with our bot: your in-game name, a link to your public AoE4World profile, and your official ratings.
  • Community matches you played: the queue, teams, civilizations, bans, results and dates.
  • Statistics worked out from those matches, such as win rates, streaks, rivals, teammates and achievements.
  • The total time you have spent in our voice channels.
  • While you're in one of our public voice channels: which channel you're in and how long you've been there. Private channels, and who is in them, are never shown.
  • Matches in progress, as they happen. Civilization picks and bans only appear once they've been revealed in Discord.

Where it comes from

Our Discord bot records community match results, account links, member names and profile pictures, and how long members spend in our voice channels. It records when members join and leave voice channels to work out time spent and to show who is in our public voice channels right now. It never records what is said.

Official ratings come from AoE4World, which publishes Age of Empires IV ratings publicly.

What we don't collect

The website never sees or stores passwords or email addresses, and uses no advertising trackers.

To understand how many people visit, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't build a profile of you and doesn't follow you across other websites. We only see totals, such as page views, popular pages, countries, browsers and which site sent visitors here.

Logging in

You can log in with your Discord account. There's no separate sign-up or password. When you log in, Discord shares your Discord ID, username and profile picture with us, plus the list of servers you're in. We only use that list to check whether you've joined The Cheese Club, and we don't store it.

Logging in sets one cookie that keeps you logged in for up to 30 days. It's only used for logging in, and you can remove it any time by logging out.

When you're logged in, the website saves things you choose yourself: the AoE4World profile you link (the same link the Discord bot uses), whether you've chosen to hide your profile, and your queue alert settings (which queues, when, quiet hours, your time zone and how you want to be told).

If you turn on queue alerts, our Discord bot may send you direct messages about queues, and the website keeps a short list of your notifications (queue alerts, matches starting, match results and new achievements) for the bell at the top of the site. Browser pop-ups only appear if you allow them, and you can switch any of this off in Settings.

Other services involved

  • Google Fonts supplies the website's fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads.
  • Discord handles logging in and serves members' profile pictures, so Discord receives your IP address when you log in or a picture is shown.
  • AoE4World is contacted by our server, not your browser, unless you follow a link to their site.
  • Cloudflare receives each page visit for our visitor statistics, as described above.
  • Railway hosts the website and its database.

Who can see it

Everything on this website is public. Anyone with the link can view it, pages can be shared, and search engines may list them.

How long we keep it

Match and voice records are kept for as long as the community runs, so rankings and match history stay complete, unless you ask us to remove them.

Your choices

  • Hide your profile. Log in and switch on "Hide my profile" in your Account, or ask us to do it for you. You'll disappear from leaderboards, member lists and player pages. Matches you played still count towards totals and civilization stats, but you'll appear as "Hidden player".
  • Delete your records. We'll remove your match, voice and account-link records from our database.
  • Correct something. Tell us if a result, name or account link is wrong.

To make a request, message a founder or moderator on our Discord server. We aim to respond within 30 days.

Age

Our community runs on Discord, so members must meet Discord's minimum age for their country.

Changes to this page

If we change how the website handles members' information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
